## Get back to the right conversation

Find a saved session, inspect its recorded ending and file changes, and follow
links between related runs where that information is available. For supported
stopped sessions, **Resume in Terminal** uses the recorded working folder. If
direct resume is unavailable, Gantry explains why and offers a copy command
where supported.

## Move through your workspace faster

The refreshed **Overview** brings recent work and agent shortcuts closer. Press
**⌘K** to search everything or narrow the palette to projects, sessions,
capabilities, or actions. Sessions retains useful filters and selection as you
move between screens.

## Inspect the setup with more confidence

- **Skill verification** avoids executing a repository's filesystem monitor;
  checksum manifests handle common line endings and binary markers.
- **Configuration changes** refuse ambiguous duplicate-key and symlink cases
  before writing. Supported edits remain previewable, backed up, and undoable.
- **MCP responses** preserve completed replies when a server exits or sends an
  oversized follow-up. Project metadata changes trigger a refresh more reliably.
- **Clearer navigation** improves sidebar legibility in light and dark modes,
  and restores the main window when you jump to a section after closing it.

## Local records. Your choice of action.

No Gantry account. No analytics telemetry. Gantry keeps its index and edit
backups on your Mac and does not upload session history to a Gantry service.
MCP checks can launch configured servers or contact selected endpoints; those
services may use their own credentials and make network requests. Coverage
varies by tool; quota is cached and cost is an API-rate estimate, not a bill.

**New:** check for updates when you ask, or opt into weekly checks. Gantry
opens downloads in your browser and never installs an update automatically.

## Download and verification

Download `Gantry-1.3.2.dmg` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` from this release. With both
files in the same folder, verify the image before installing:

```sh
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt
```

**SHA-256:** `5376cbe314e1c2675e75426f1b5300a9b07a8cd6a24a24bf0600b40536361a93`

A checksum identifies the downloaded bytes; it does not authenticate their
publisher or mean Apple has notarized the app.
